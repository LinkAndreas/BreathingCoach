# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Everyone taking part in
issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to help keep the space respectful and harassment
free.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and constructive; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask questions before assuming intent.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it the way you would want to receive it.
- Respect that maintainers volunteer their time and may say no to a change.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal, political or identity-based attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing issues and reviews.
- Sharing other people's health data or clinical records in issues. This project deals with
  physiological measurements — never attach real patient data to a report; redact or synthesize it.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer via GitHub — open a
[private report](https://github.com/LinkAndreas/BreathingCoach/security/advisories/new) or contact
[@LinkAndreas](https://github.com/LinkAndreas) directly. Reports are handled confidentially and
reviewed as promptly as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or reject comments, commits, code and issues that violate this Code of
Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening or harmful. Where practical, a warning is given first.
